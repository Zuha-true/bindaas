# Bindaas Account Check roadmap

The current account check is a local comparison and checklist. It does not query social platforms or send profile handles to a service.

Potential future work, requiring a separate design and privacy review:

- Reverse image or face matching
- AI-generated face detection
- Account-age or follower-count scraping
- Platform behavioral signals
- A risk percentage
- A browser extension
- Monitoring for new lookalike accounts
